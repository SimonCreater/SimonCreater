## 🇺🇸 Profile

### Undergraduate Researcher @ DIA Lab (Data Intelligence & Applications), Inha University  
**Dec 2024 – Present**

As an undergraduate researcher, I am currently participating in the "Regional Intelligent Innovation Talent Cultivation Project" funded by IITP under MSIT.

My research focuses on the *Classification of False and True Defects in Semiconductor Packaging Based on E-Map*, aiming to enhance quality control efficiency through advanced AI integration.

### Technical Focus

**Defect Detection & Classification**  
Developing high-precision models to identify micro-defects in QFN and LGA packages. A Faster R-CNN baseline has been established, and the SOTA RT-DETR model is currently being fine-tuned to improve detection performance.

**Industrial AI Application**  
Automating defect detection and minimizing false alarm rates to improve reliability in semiconductor manufacturing environments.
